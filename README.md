# Track Show/Hide

A LUNA Add On for showing and hiding tracks, buses and folders in the Timeline and Mixer.

Needs LUNA 3.1.2 or newer.

## Install

Download `Track-Show-Hide-<version>.lunaaddon` from the latest
[release](../../releases/latest) and double-click it. It opens from LUNA's Add Ons menu, under
Browsers. LUNA may need a relaunch before a newly installed Add On shows in the menu.

## Use

- Click a track to show or hide it. Shift-click another track to do the same to every track
  in between. Drag across tracks to show or hide them all at once.
- Show All, Hide All, Show Selected Only, Hide Selected and Invert act on the whole list.
  The selected tracks are the ones selected in LUNA.
- Type in the filter box to narrow the list by name or type. Show/Hide Matching then acts on
  the tracks that match.
- Presets: the built-in ones show one type of track (Audio, Instrument, MIDI, Buses,
  Folders). Click "+ Save" to save the tracks shown now, or the tracks selected in LUNA, as
  your own preset. Click a preset to show only its tracks; Shift-click to add them to what is
  shown. Your presets are saved with the session. Click Edit to rename, update or delete them.
